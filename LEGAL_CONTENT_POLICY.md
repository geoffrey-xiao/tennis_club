# Legal Content Policy

This project must follow copyright-safe content rules.

## News Articles

Allowed:

- Original summaries
- Short commentary
- Facts such as scores, match dates, player names, and tournament names
- Source links

Not allowed:

- Copying full articles
- Copying large paragraphs
- Translating full articles from another publisher
- Scraping paywalled content
- Presenting another publisher’s reporting as our own

## Images

Allowed:

- Self-created graphics
- Royalty-free images with proper license
- Public domain images
- Licensed images
- Placeholder graphics

Not allowed:

- Scraping official tournament photography
- Rehosting Getty/AP/Reuters images without license
- Using player images from random websites

## Video

Allowed:

- Embedding official videos when the platform allows embedding
- Linking to official highlights
- Creating original commentary videos

Not allowed:

- Downloading and reuploading match footage
- Using copyrighted broadcast clips without permission

## Data

Allowed:

- Publicly known facts such as scores and schedules
- Official data with attribution
- Licensed APIs

Not allowed:

- Scraping paid databases
- Republishing restricted statistical feeds

## User-Generated Content

Future community features should require users to confirm:

- They own the content they upload
- They will not upload copyrighted match footage
- The platform may remove infringing content
